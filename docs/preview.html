Movable icons

The eight circular toggles are a config-driven grid. Long-press any one to enter arrange mode, drag to reorder, and the order writes back to shell.json. Add or remove from a palette of about twenty actions, or write your own.

Expand in place

Tapping the chevron on Wi-Fi grows that tile downward into a network list inside the panel. No page navigation, ever — the sheet just gets taller.

One red

Accent appears on the media transport and nowhere else by default. Everything structural is carried by greyscale and inverted fill.
